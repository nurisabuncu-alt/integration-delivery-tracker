Objectives and key results Q4 2026

Linked projects take their weight from allocated quarter hours divided by one person's quarter capacity (weekdays × 6 h). That total does not have to be 100%. Result is achievement times weight. Manual rows keep the weight you type.

Task and phase completion can be edited after sync.

Quick mode: Type + workflows (Std/Cpx) + Dependency + Weeks. Meetings = (weeks − 2) × 0.5 h weekly calls + 1 h kickoff. Blank weeks keeps the 20% overhead. Premium and Misc load each month from Rocketlane time entries; edit a month to override. The Hours field is the package budget. Other uses one number per month, and the quarter total is the sum of those months.

Work %: Quarter hours ÷ sum of quarter hours (per user / team).

Import RL CSV: Rocketlane time report export (TrackedTime in hours). Seeds projects from actuals; Std workflows are reverse-estimated — review Type, Dependency, and flow counts.
