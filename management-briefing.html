The quarter, in one place

Rocketlane holds the projects. The Integration Delivery Tracker turns that portfolio into the three questions we answer before we commit the quarter.

Estimator

Can we staff the work?

Hours and story points by person, placed in the quarter where the work happens.

Compliance

Are we delivering it properly?

The controls due at this phase, the evidence, and the next action on each project.

OKRs

Are we on the outcomes?

Each person’s plan becomes their weight. Progress is scored only for this quarter.

Rocketlane projects→ Estimator→ Compliance→ OKRs
Same projects. Same quarter. Three reads for the room.

03 / 04

See delivery risk before go-live

Every in-scope project carries the same controls, from the plan through closure. The score uses only what is due for the phase the project is in.

Compliant · 90–100 Due controls are complete, with evidence on the project.
Watch · 70–89 Delivery is moving, with gaps still open in this phase.
At risk · below 70 Or any critical control already overdue. The portfolio leads with these.
Each project shows one next action. Dates, notes, and evidence stay with the record.

04 / 04

Score the outcomes against the plan

A linked project’s weight is its allocated quarter hours divided by one person’s quarter capacity. That total does not have to be 100%. Progress counts only the phases that belong in this quarter.

W
Weight comes from the estimate Each project is its hours divided by the quarter's available hours. Adding one does not rebalance the others. Objectives that are not a delivery project keep the weight we type.
A
Achievement is this quarter only Rocketlane progress counts for the phases in this quarter. Later work does not move the score.
R
Result is the two combined Progress toward the goal, times the weight. A key result with no phases this quarter stays unscored.
The plan sets the weight. Delivery progress sets the score.
